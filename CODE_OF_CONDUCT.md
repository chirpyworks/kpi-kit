# Community conduct

Discuss the code and the evidence respectfully. Constructive disagreement is welcome; harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, doxxing, spam and disclosure of private data are not.

Give people enough context to reproduce an issue. Explain limitations without belittling newcomers. Do not represent generated examples or invented results as someone else's experience.

Maintainers may ask for edits, lock a discussion, remove unsafe content or restrict participation when needed to protect the project. For a conduct concern, use an available private maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting tools; do not repost private information in a public issue.
